The video explains how to use the grade sheet for the professional discussion element of the Digital and Technology Solutions apprenticeship. It shows how assessors enter step‑marked grades for each competency, how pass and distinction outcomes are calculated, and how the same marks also produce a numeric grade for the degree. It outlines that all pass criteria must be met, distinction requires higher‑level responses, and some competencies can only be awarded at pass level. The sheet then automatically works out the EPA result and the degree mark.
