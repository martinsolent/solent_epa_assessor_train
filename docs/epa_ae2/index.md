The EPA portfolio requires apprentices to compile six workplace-based evidence items that demonstrate their knowledge, skills and behaviours across four themes: Underlying Principles, Technical Solutions, Innovation & Response, and Legal & Ethical Practice.

Evidence should focus on real work projects, clearly explain your personal contribution, be mapped to relevant KSBs, and include reflection and supporting evidence where appropriate. The portfolio is then used to support a professional discussion with an independent assessor as part of the End-Point Assessment.
